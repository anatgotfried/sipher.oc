Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to sipher.oc ("we," "our," or "us"). We provide a digital assistant service designed to help you manage your schedule and tasks. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our digital assistant services, we may collect and process specific types of data, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Calendar Data: Event titles, times, and descriptions (strictly for scheduling purposes).
  • Task Data: To-do lists and task details you provide to the assistant.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the purpose of operating and improving our digital assistant service, including:

  • Managing your appointments and calendar events.
  • Organizing and prioritizing your tasks.
  • Providing personalized productivity insights.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your calendar and task information is encrypted and accessed only by the automated system to perform requested actions.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices or how we handle your data, please contact us at: support@sipher.site.
